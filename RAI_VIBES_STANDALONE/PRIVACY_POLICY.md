# Privacy Policy for RAI VIBES & RAI SENTINEL

**Last Updated:** March 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how **RAI VIBES** and **RAI SENTINEL** (the "Bots") collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services on Discord.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
To provide music playback, moderation, and verification features, the Bot processes minimal and necessary Discord data:
* **User Identifiers:** Discord User ID and Username (to identify song requests, favorites, and verification status).
* **Guild Identifiers:** Discord Guild (Server) ID, Channel IDs, and Role IDs (to configure server-specific settings, prefixes, and verification roles).
* **Temporary Data:** Voice channel state (to join and leave voice channels when requested).

**We DO NOT collect or store:**
* Passwords or payment information.
* Personal messages, DMs, or voice call audio streams.

---

### 2. How We Use Information
The collected information is used solely to:
* Stream music and maintain queue systems requested by users.
* Assign verification and community roles.
* Manage moderation logs and support tickets created in your server.

---

### 3. Data Retention & Storage
* Server settings, verification status, and music favorites are stored securely and locally.
* We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties.

---

### 4. Data Deletion
Server owners or users may request data deletion at any time by removing the Bot from their server or contacting the bot developer team.

---

### 5. Compliance with Discord Developer Policy
Our Bots strictly adhere to Discord's Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy.

---

### 6. Contact
For any privacy inquiries or data requests, please contact our developer team via our Discord community server.
